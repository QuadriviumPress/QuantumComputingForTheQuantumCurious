*Quantum Computing for the Quantum Curious* is an open-access book by
**Ciaran Hughes, Joshua Isaacson, Anastasia Perry, Ranbel F. Sun, and
Jessica Turner**, published by Springer in 2021. Written for high-school
students and anyone curious about quantum physics, it introduces
superposition, qubits, beam splitters, Stern–Gerlach experiments, quantum
cryptography, quantum gates, entanglement, quantum teleportation, and
quantum algorithms — with interactive worksheets.

This MyST Markdown edition adapts the printed book with documented editorial corrections: all
equations are live LaTeX, all figures are the original book artwork, and
the chapter-ending worksheets are included.
